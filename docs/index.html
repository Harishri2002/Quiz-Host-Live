Round Types

8 Unique Event Formats

Mix and match for the perfect show flow.

Q&A Round
Classic Q&A

Text, image, or audio questions. Teams answer in turn or buzz in.

Identify Round
Image & Sound Identification

Show a blurred image or play a sound clip. Teams identify it before the reveal.

Buzzer Round
First-to-Buzz

First team to buzz in gets to answer. Wrong? Others get a chance.

Lightning Round
Rapid-fire per Team

Consecutive questions directed at specific teams. Configure per-team counts and timers.

Wipeout Round
Wagering

Teams commit points before answering. One wrong answer wipes their wager entirely.

Rapid Fire Duel
Head-to-Head

Pick 2 teams for a showdown. Both race through questions with a shared timer.

Card Flip Round
Challenge Cards

Teams pick from a custom card grid. Each card hides a mini-quiz challenge.

Master Questions
Multi-point Deduction

Teams can ask for hints. The more hints used, the fewer points available.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use my own images and audio?
Yes! In the question editor, switch the Media Type toggle to Image or Sound and upload any local file. Files are embedded as base64 in the save file for portability.
How do I uninstall on Windows?
Open Control Panel → Programs → Uninstall a program, find "Quiz-Host Live" and click Uninstall. You can also use Settings → Apps on Windows 11.
Can I run it without internet?
Fully offline. All data is stored locally. An internet connection is only required to load Google Fonts in the UI.
Can I use it with a projector?
Yes — run the app on your laptop and extend/duplicate to the projector screen. Use View → Full Screen for a clean audience display.
Can I save and resume a game later?
Yes. Press Ctrl+S or click "Save Game" at any time. Your quiz, sequences, and scores are all saved to your local .qmg file.
How do I import a quiz someone shared?
On the launch screen, click "Import" and select the .json file. The game loads immediately with all settings intact.
How do I reset scores mid-game?
Go to Game Info panel (in Setup) and scroll to the bottom. There's a red "Reset Scores" button that zeros out all team scores instantly.
